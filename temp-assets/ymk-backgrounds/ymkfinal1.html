Who We Are

Building Saudi Arabia's Future

نبني مستقبل المملكة

YMK General Contracting Est. is a premier construction and contracting firm headquartered in Dammam, Saudi Arabia. Founded on principles of excellence, integrity, and precision, we deliver world-class construction solutions across the Kingdom.

Our expertise spans general contracting, infrastructure development, interior fit-out, MEP works, and specialized road and pavement construction — all executed in alignment with Saudi Arabia's ambitious Vision 2030 programme.

With over a decade of operation, 85+ completed projects, and 60+ satisfied clients across the Eastern Province, YMK stands as a trusted partner for both private developers and government bodies.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

الأسئلة الشائعة

What areas do you serve in Saudi Arabia? ▾
YMK General Contracting Est. is headquartered in Dammam and primarily serves the Eastern Province — including Al Khobar, Dhahran, Jubail, and surrounding cities. We also undertake projects across the Kingdom for large-scale engagements.
What types of projects do you handle? ▾
We specialise in general construction (residential and commercial), infrastructure and civil works, interior fit-out, MEP systems, road and pavement works, and end-to-end project management.
Are you aligned with Saudi Vision 2030? ▾
Yes. YMK is actively contributing to Vision 2030 goals by supporting giga-project construction in the Eastern Province, employing and developing Saudi national talent, and adopting sustainable building practices.
How do I request a quote? ▾
You can reach us by filling in the contact form on this page, calling +966 57 010 6535, emailing ymk888@outlook.sa, or messaging us directly on WhatsApp. We aim to respond to all enquiries within one business day.
How long has YMK been operating? ▾
YMK General Contracting Est. has been operating for over 10 years, completing 85+ projects and serving 60+ satisfied clients across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
Do you handle government and private projects? ▾
Yes. We work with both private developers and government bodies, delivering construction and infrastructure projects to Saudi and international standards.
